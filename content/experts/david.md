## Core knowledge
- **A contract needs:** offer, acceptance, consideration and clear terms; read termination, liability and payment clauses first.
- **Get it in writing:** emails and signed documents are evidence.
- **Employment:** rights differ by country; termination usually requires notice and a fair process.
- **Limitation periods:** claims expire; don't wait.
- **Before signing:** check scope, payment terms, IP ownership, non-compete, governing law.
